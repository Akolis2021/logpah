Who We Are

A sisterhood built on love and unity

Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation (LOGPAH) is a women-led initiative focused on empowering young girls and women through holistic support from school outreaches to mentorship, welfare care, and community advocacy across Lagos and beyond.

What began as a small circle of women with a shared conviction has grown, over ten deliberate years, into a movement one that shows up, again and again, for the girls and women who need us most.

What We Stand For

Five pillars, one purpose

Every programme we run traces back to one of five commitments the same five that have carried LOGPAH through a decade of service.

01

Empowerment

Equipping young girls and women with skills, confidence, and opportunity to shape their own futures.

02

Self Discovery

Guiding every woman we meet toward her own sense of identity, purpose, and worth.

03

Motherless Care

Standing in the gap for motherless children with consistent, compassionate support.

04

Less-Privileged Support

Reaching destitute and less-privileged members of our community with tangible relief.

05

Marital Wellness

Counsel and community for women navigating courtship, marriage, and the seasons between.
