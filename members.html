The Sisterhood

Meet the women behind LOGPAH

A foundation is only ever as strong as the women who show up for it. Here's a look at the council that steers LOGPAH, and the growing sisterhood standing behind them.

Founder & CEO

“We may not be able to change the whole world at once, but together, we can change someone's world.”

Mrs. Beatrice Modupe Aladegbola

Founder & Chief Executive Officer, LOGPAH

Mrs. Beatrice Modupe Aladegbola is a compassionate humanitarian, visionary leader, and the founder of Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation, established in August 2016. Originally from Ikere-Ekiti, Nigeria, and now residing in the United Kingdom, she has dedicated herself to creating opportunities, restoring hope, and supporting vulnerable individuals and families.

She studied at St. Peter's Primary School, Akure; Olodi Primary School, Warri; St. Matthias High School, Akure; and Ijapo High School, Akure, before attending Lagos State Polytechnic, Ikorodu, where she studied Mass Communication. Through her leadership, the foundation has supported children, widows, single mothers, elderly people, and vulnerable members of society through humanitarian outreaches, educational initiatives, health programmes, food support, and community empowerment.

Guided by her belief that when we unite our resources, talents, time, and hearts, we can create meaningful and lasting change, she continues to champion grassroots empowerment and touch lives across Nigeria and the diaspora. Read her full story, accomplishments, and personal message on Our Story.

The Executive Council

The women who steer the vision

Each member of the council volunteers her time, wisdom, and warmth to keep LOGPAH's programmes running, from outreach planning to welfare care.

Mrs. Adenuga Idowu Oluwabunmi

General Secretary
B.Ed Electrical Vocational Education M.Sc in View Entrepreneur (20+ yrs)

I am a dedicated, responsible, and committed woman who believes strongly in the power of unity and mutual support among women.

As General Secretary, I am responsible for keeping accurate records, coordinating communication, documenting meetings and activities, and supporting the smooth administration of the group. I also believe that effective leadership requires listening to others, respecting different opinions, and working together towards common goals.

My goal is to serve with integrity, humility, dedication, and transparency, while helping to make LOGPAH stronger and more impactful. In addition to my administrative leadership, I am an entrepreneur focusing on snacks and cake baking with two decades of experience (founder of A-Series Creative World Ventures).

Adadey Ngozi

Welfare Officer
Educator & Businesswoman Welfare & Care Coordinator

Adadey Ngozi is an educator and businesswoman who is passionate about supporting people, promoting well-being, and contributing positively to her community. She is a caring, responsible, and dedicated individual who believes in teamwork, compassion, and making a meaningful difference in the lives of others.

As the Welfare Officer of Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation, she contributes to the welfare and well-being of members and beneficiaries of the Foundation. She helps identify welfare needs, supports members during challenging times, coordinates welfare initiatives, and promotes unity, care, and mutual support among members.

Her experience in education and business has strengthened her skills in communication, organisation, leadership, problem-solving, teamwork, and working effectively with people from different backgrounds. She brings empathy, commitment, and a strong sense of responsibility to her role in the Foundation.

Modinat Abimbola Adaranijo-Adesoji

Foundational Member
Healthcare Professional Iya Suna Adini General 📍 UK & Lagos, Nigeria

My name is Modinat Abimbola Adaranijo-Adesoji (The Iya Suna Adini General, Orile Agege Lagos Central Mosque). I am a mother blessed with children and a daughter to Chief S.A. Adaranijo. Born in August 1974 in Agege Local Government, Lagos State, Nigeria, I completed my primary and secondary education in Lagos before attending higher institution in Ibadan, Oyo State. I am God-fearing, committed to humanity, and a woman who takes immense pleasure in caring for others, embracing and respecting all people without discrimination.

Prior to relocating to the United Kingdom, I worked with the Lagos State Government at the Marriage Registry Department, Ikeja Local Government. I currently work within the Healthcare system in the UK, where my role goes beyond providing care—I am an essential part of a system built on compassion, dignity, respect, and the preservation of human life.

I serve as an advocate, a caregiver, a source of emotional support, and, most importantly, a voice for individuals who may not always be able to speak up for themselves. My responsibility is to ensure that every person in my care is treated with dignity, kindness, and respect, regardless of their background, condition, or circumstances.
