A Notable Encounter

Community Health Outreach & Engagement

As part of our commitment to improving the wellbeing of individuals and communities, Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation carried out an outreach at Gbagada General Hospital, Lagos.

During the outreach, our team had the privilege of meeting with the Honourable Commissioner for Health, Lagos State. The encounter provided an opportunity to share our passion for community service and the work we are doing to improve the lives of vulnerable people.

Impressed by our commitment, the Honourable Commissioner invited representatives of LOGPAH to his office to formally present our organisation, our mission, and our vision for the future.
