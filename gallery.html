A Notable Encounter

Community Health Outreach & Engagement

As part of our commitment to improving the wellbeing of individuals and communities, Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation carried out an outreach at Gbagada General Hospital, Lagos.

During the outreach, our team had the privilege of meeting with the Honourable Commissioner for Health, Lagos State. The encounter provided an opportunity to share our passion for community service and the work we are doing to improve the lives of vulnerable people.

Impressed by our commitment, the Honourable Commissioner invited representatives of LOGPAH to his office to formally present our organisation, our mission, and our vision for the future.

Community Outreach & Impact

Promoting Good Hygiene Among Children

As part of our commitment to building healthier communities, our team visited Ayangburen Primary School, Ikorodu, where we engaged the pupils in an educational session on the importance of personal hygiene and healthy daily habits.

Through this interactive session led by the General Secretary of the foundation, Mrs Oluwabunmi Adenuga, we taught the children practical ways to care for themselves, maintain good hygiene and protect their health.

We believe that educating children about healthy habits from an early age helps them grow into healthier, more confident individuals and contributes to a healthier community.
