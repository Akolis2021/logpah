A Decade of Elegance and Excellence

The story behind the sisterhood

From a small circle of women with a shared conviction to a foundation that shows up, again and again — this is how LOGPAH came to be.

Our Journey

Ten years, four seasons

The Beginning

A shared conviction

LOGPAH started small — a circle of women who believed that no girl or woman should have to face hardship alone. What began as informal support among friends soon found a name, a purpose, and a crest of its own.

Finding Our Voice

From circle to community

As word spread, more women joined the cause. Outreaches grew from one-off gestures into structured programmes built around empowerment, self-discovery, and care for the motherless and less-privileged.

Growing the Sisterhood

Structure, and a growing family

An executive council took shape to steward the vision. LOGPAH's signature blue and gold became a familiar, welcome sight at schools, hospitals, and community events across Lagos.

A Decade of Distinction

Ten years, and still showing up

Today, LOGPAH marks ten years of service under the theme “A Decade of Elegance and Excellence” — honouring the past, and building toward an even bigger next decade.

Leadership

“We didn't set out to build an organisation — we set out to show up for one another. Ten years later, that is still the whole story.”

LOGPAH Leadership

Executive Council

Under her stewardship, LOGPAH has grown from a small gathering of like-minded women into a structured foundation with an active executive council, a growing membership base, and outreach programmes that reach schools, hospitals, and communities across Lagos — all held together by one guiding value: love and unity.
