ABOUT US

Who We Are
Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation is a community-focused organisation founded on the principles of compassion, service, empowerment and giving back to society. We are committed to supporting vulnerable individuals, families and communities while creating opportunities that promote dignity, hope and a better quality of life.

Our Beginning
Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation was founded in August 2016 with a vision to bring people together to make a positive difference in the lives of others. What began with a passion to serve has grown into a foundation committed to reaching people in need and making a meaningful impact within our communities.

Our Work & Achievements

Over the years, we have carried out various outreach and community-support initiatives, including:

* 🏠 Visiting motherless homes, including the Red Cross Motherless Home in Yaba, Lagos, and Mother Theresa Motherless Home in Alapere, Ketu.

* 🍲 Feeding the destitute around Ketu Bus Stop.

* 🎀 Supporting the girl child through our Pad a Girl Child initiative at Ikosi Junior Secondary School.
* ❤️ Supporting widows, widowers and single mothers by distributing food items and essential supplies at the palace of the Oba Jagunmolu of Bariga, Oba Gbolahan Timson.
* 🤝 Community outreach and support, providing practical assistance to people and families facing difficult circumstances.
* ⁠Visit to Gbagada General Hospital to support patients with cash for their medications 

Our Values

Compassion • Service • Unity • Empowerment • Integrity • Love • Community Impact

Our Vision for the Future

OUR MILESTONES

Since our establishment in August 2016, Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation has remained committed to serving humanity, supporting vulnerable people and bringing hope to communities.

Our journey has been filled with memorable moments, meaningful outreach programmes and opportunities to touch lives.

2016 — The Beginning

Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation was founded in August 2016 with a vision to bring people together to serve humanity, support vulnerable individuals and contribute positively to our communities.

Caring for Children

Over the years, our team has visited different motherless homes, including the Red Cross Motherless Home, Yaba, Lagos, and Mother Theresa Motherless Home, Alapere, Ketu.

These visits have given us the opportunity to spend time with the children, provide support and remind them that they are loved and cared for.

Supporting the Elderly

We have also extended our care to the elderly through our visit to MASC Care Home, Abule Egba, Lagos.

Our visit was an opportunity to bring companionship, kindness and support to elderly members of our community.

Feeding the Destitute

Recognising the challenges faced by people living in vulnerable circumstances, our foundation has organised feeding outreach programmes, including providing food to the destitute around Ketu Bus Stop.

Empowering the Girl Child

Through our Pad a Girl Child initiative at Ikosi Junior Secondary School, we provided sanitary support to young girls and helped promote dignity, confidence and continued participation in education.

Supporting Education

We believe that education is an important foundation for a better future. As part of our community initiatives, we distributed stationery and notebooks to pupils at Ayangburen Primary School, Ikorodu.

Supporting Widows, Widowers & Single Mothers

Our foundation has also reached out to vulnerable families by distributing foodstuffs and essential support to widows, widowers and single mothers at the palace of the Oba Jagunmolu of Bariga, Oba Gbolahan Timson.

This initiative reflects our commitment to standing with families who may be experiencing difficult circumstances.

Community Health Outreach — Gbagada General Hospital

One of our memorable community engagements was our outreach at Gbagada General Hospital, Lagos.

During the outreach, our team had the privilege of meeting with the Honourable Commissioner for Health, Lagos State. This provided an opportunity for us to share our passion for community service and the work of our foundation.

Following the encounter, the Honourable Commissioner invited representatives of Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation to his office to formally brief him about our mission, community initiatives and vision for the future.

We are grateful for opportunities such as this that allow us to connect with key stakeholders and explore meaningful partnerships for greater community impact.

Our Journey Continues

These milestones represent only a part of our journey. Every visit, donation, outreach and helping hand represents our commitment to making a difference.

As we look towards the future, Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation remains committed to reaching more communities, supporting more vulnerable people and creating initiatives that bring hope and positive change.

Our journey began with a vision.
Our impact continues through service.
And our future is filled with greater plans and greater hope.

Ladies Of Greater Plans And Hope Foundation
Serving with Compassion • Empowering Lives • Giving Hope
